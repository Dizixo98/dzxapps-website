Privacy Policy

Last updated: 05.08.2026

DZX Apps ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information MainzIQ stores, how it is used, how it is protected, and how you can control or delete it.

Information We Store

MainzIQ stores the information you add to the app, including vehicles, service history, mileage, images and settings. If you sign in with Google or Apple, your data is securely stored in Firebase Authentication and Firebase Cloud Firestore for authentication, backup and synchronization across your devices. If you have MainzIQ Premium, service images are also securely synchronized between your devices.

How We Use Your Data

Your information is used solely to provide the app's features, including vehicle management, service history, statistics, export, Premium features and synchronization. For vehicles added using automatic lookup, MainzIQ may retrieve updated information about the next roadworthiness inspection from the Norwegian Public Roads Administration once per day.

Data Sharing

MainzIQ does not sell or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. When you sign in with Google or Apple, MainzIQ uses Firebase Authentication and Firebase Cloud Firestore for authentication, cloud storage and synchronization. When automatic vehicle lookup is used, your registration number is sent to the Norwegian Public Roads Administration's public API to retrieve vehicle information.

Data Security

Your data is stored either locally on your device or securely in Firebase Cloud Firestore when synchronization is enabled. If you have MainzIQ Premium, service images may also be securely stored in the cloud. All communication with our cloud services is encrypted. While we use industry-standard security measures, no system can guarantee complete security.

Cloud Storage and Synchronization

When you sign in with Google or Apple, your vehicles, service history, mileage and other information are securely synchronized across your devices. If you have MainzIQ Premium, service images are also securely synchronized between your devices.

Important:
Without MainzIQ Premium, service images are stored locally on your device only and are not included in cloud synchronization or backup. If you uninstall the app or lose your device, locally stored images cannot be recovered.

Your Rights and Data Deletion

You can manage or delete your data at any time:

  • Delete individual vehicles or service records directly within the app — this permanently removes them from your device and, if signed in, from the cloud.
  • Delete your account directly from the account settings within the app.
  • Sign out of your Google or Apple account in the app to stop future cloud synchronization.
  • Uninstall the app to remove all locally stored data from your device.
  • Contact us at the email below if you have questions about privacy, data processing, or need help deleting your account or cloud data.

Children's Privacy

MainzIQ is not directed at children and has no specific age restriction for general use. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can delete it.

Changes to This Policy

These Terms and the Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Continued use of MainzIQ after changes have been published constitutes acceptance of the updated terms.

Contact

If you have questions about privacy, data processing or would like your account or cloud data deleted, you can contact DZX Apps at Support@DZXapps.com. You can also delete your account directly from the account settings within the app.
